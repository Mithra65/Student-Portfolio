## SafeHer – AI-Powered Predictive Women Safety Ecosystem
- **Problem:** Traditional women safety systems mainly depend on manually triggering an SOS after danger occurs.
- **Solution:** An AI-powered system that detects possible danger using voice stress, motion, route deviation, risky locations, and other signals.
- **Technologies:** AI/ML, GPS/GNSS, voice and motion analysis, blockchain
- **My contribution:** Contributed to the project idea, system design, feature planning, and predictive safety workflow.
- **What I learned:** Learned about predictive safety systems, AI-based risk detection, emergency response workflows, and system design.
- **Future improvement:** Improve prediction accuracy by training the system with larger and more diverse real-world datasets.
